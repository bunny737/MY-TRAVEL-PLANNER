## 1. Route facts that agree across several partner pages
- Vizag to Araku: about 115 km, roughly 3.5 to 4.5 hours by road over the Ananthagiri ghat road. One page says 3 to 3.5 hours, another 3 to 4. VERIFY before printing exact hours.
- Train alternative: the Vizag to Kirandul passenger line passes Borra and Araku, about 4 to 5 hours (their figure, no timetable). Scenic-coach tickets are separate. VERIFY timetable on IRCTC.
- Best months: October to February (cooler, clearer). Monsoon July to September (green, fuller falls, wet and slippery). March to June warm.
- Ghat road: sharp bends, best driven in daylight, start early, fuel up in Vizag, carry cash (patchy network and UPI in the hills).
- Borra Caves sit on the way up from Vizag (about 90 km from Vizag, elevation about 705 m by their page). Katiki Falls is a short forest walk from the road near Borra; vehicles cannot reach the final approach; a local jeep ride and entry fee may apply (₹200 per person each for Katiki and Vanjangi jeeps on one page, cash on the spot, undated, VERIFY).

## 4. Attractions (what each place is; fees and hours are NOT given by the partner and are never printed)
- **Borra Caves**: limestone caves in the Ananthagiri hills; stalactites, stalagmites, a Shiva-linga-like formation; lit path with steps and rails; about 80 m deep (their figure); open about 10 AM to 5 PM by their page, counters may pause at midday (VERIFY); allow 45 to 90 min; grippy shoes, damp floor; no restrooms or drinking water inside; watch for monkeys near the entrance; camera fees may apply. Discovery date and age claims are unsourced: do not print.
- **Katiki Falls**: about 50 ft fall into a pool, reached by a short forest walk from the road; flow best July to December, reduced March to June; best in the morning; local guide advised; avoid after heavy rain.
- **Chaparai Falls**: wide, shallow cascade over flat rock near Araku town; strongest after the monsoon (July to November); grippy shoes, change of clothes, bring snacks and water (few shops); watch children near slippery edges.
- **Padmapuram Gardens**: botanical garden near Araku town with tree-top huts, a toy train, lawns and flower beds; about 45 to 60 min; ticket small, huts and train extra; cash advised; mornings are fresher.
- **Araku Coffee Museum and coffee house**: shows coffee growing, drying, roasting and processing; café and shop; about 9 AM to 5:30 PM "usually" (VERIFY); about 45 to 60 min. Who runs it: not stated by the partner (they mention a state tourism ticket: do NOT say who operates it).
- **Tribal Museum**: displays of tribal life, tools, crafts, a handicraft shop; live Dhimsa dance on some days at set times (times not given); camera ticket may apply; about 45 to 60 min.
- **Viewpoints**: Dumuku (roadside stop on the way up; spelled "Damuku" on one page), Galikonda (wide ghats views, fog can roll in fast), Madagada (sunrise), Pine Valley / Araku Pinery (tall pine trees).
- **Pets and Wings Araku Valley**: a visitor stop on the ghat road (30 to 45 min); nature of the attraction not described: keep to the name and "visitor stop".
- **Lambasingi**: small hill village, about 100 km from Vizag, 3 to 3.5 h (their figure), at about 1,000 m; foggy winter mornings, frost on some days (not guaranteed); sights: village viewpoint, Thajangi Reservoir (sunrise), strawberry and apple patches (seasonal, months not given), coffee and pepper estates, Kothapalli Falls (best in and after the monsoon). Few stays, mostly basic; shops are limited, carry fuel and snacks; no direct train. Claims like "coldest village" or "close to 0 degrees" are unsourced: do not print.
- **Vizag**: RK Beach promenade (sunset, evening walks), Kailasagiri hilltop park (statue, gardens, ropeway option, 2 to 3 h), Rushikonda Beach, Submarine and Aircraft museums, temples. No hours or fees given.
- **Maredumilli** (Papikonda range): Jalatarangini and other seasonal falls, bamboo and teak forest trails, forest drive on the Rampachodavaram corridor, treehouse and forest rest-house stays when available.
